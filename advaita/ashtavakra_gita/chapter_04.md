# Verse 4.1

## Sanskrit
अष्टावक्र उवाच 
हन्तात्मज्ञस्य धीरस्य
खेलतो भोगलीलया।
न हि संसारवाहीकै-
र्मूढैः सह समानता॥

## IAST
asht'aavakra uvaacha 
hantaatmajnyasya dheerasya
khelato bhogaleelayaa.
na hi samsaaravaaheekai-
rmood'haih saha samaanataa..

## Translation
Ashtavakra said: The wise person of self-knowledge, playing the game of worldly enjoyment, bears no resemblance whatever to samsara’s bewildered beasts of burden.

# Verse 4.2

## Sanskrit
यत् पदं प्रेप्सवो दीनाः
शक्राद्याः सर्वदेवताः।
अहो तत्र स्थितो योगी न
हर्षमुपगच्छति॥

## IAST
yat padam prepsavo deenaah
shakraadyaah sarvadevataah.
aho tatra sthito yogee na
harshamupagachchhati..

## Translation
Truly the yogi feels no excitement even at being established in that state which all the Devas from Indra down yearn for disconsolately.

---

# Verse 4.3

## Sanskrit
तज्ज्ञस्य पुण्यपापाभ्यां
स्पर्शो ह्यन्तर्न जायते।
न ह्याकाशस्य धूमेन
दृश्यमानापि सङ्गतिः॥

## IAST
tajjnyasya punyapaapaabhyaam
sparsho hyantarna jaayate.
na hyaakaashasya dhoomena
drishyamaanaapi sangatih..

## Translation
He who has known That is untouched within by good deeds or bad, just as space is not touched by smoke, however much it may appear to be.

---

# Verse 4.4

## Sanskrit
आत्मैवेदं जगत्सर्वं
ज्ञातं येन महात्मना।
यदृच्छया वर्तमानं
तं निषेद्धुं क्षमेत कः॥

## IAST
aatmaivedam jagatsarvam
nyaatam yena mahaatmanaa.
yadrichchhayaa vartamaanam
tam nisheddhum kshameta kah..

## Translation
Who can prevent the great-souled person who has known this whole world as himself from living as he pleases?

---

# Verse 4.5

## Sanskrit
आब्रह्मस्तंबपर्यन्ते
भूतग्रामे चतुर्विधे।
विज्ञस्यैव हि सामर्थ्य-
मिच्छानिच्छाविवर्जने॥

## IAST
aabrahmastambaparyante
bhootagraame chaturvidhe.
vijnyasyaiva hi saamarthya-
michchhaanichchhaavivarjane..

## Translation
Of all four categories of beings, from Brahma down to the last clump of grass, only the man of knowledge is capable of eliminating desire and aversion.

---

# Verse 4.6

## Sanskrit
आत्मानमद्वयं कश्चिज्-
जानाति जगदीश्वरं।
यद् वेत्ति तत्स कुरुते
न भयं तस्य कुत्रचित्॥

## IAST
aatmaanamadvayam kashchij-
yaanaati jagadeeshvaram.
yad vetti tatsa kurute
na bhayam tasya kutrachit..

## Translation
Rare is the man who knows himself as the nondual Lord of the world, and he who knows this is not afraid of anything.
