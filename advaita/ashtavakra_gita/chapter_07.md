subtitle: "Tranquil and Boundless Ocean of the Self"
synopsis: "Janaka says that the knower of the self is one who is firmly poised in the expansive or limitless experience and sees only one and therefore the world appearances create no impact either favourable or unfavourable in him; nor do they generate attraction or repulsion towards them."
---

# Verse 7.1

## Sanskrit
मय्यनंतमहांभोधौ
विश्वपोत इतस्ततः।
भ्रमति स्वांतवातेन न
ममास्त्यसहिष्णुता॥

## IAST
mayyanantamahaambhodhau
vishvapota itastatah.
bhramati svaantavaatena na
mamaastyasahishnutaa..

## Translation
Janaka said: In the infinite ocean of myself the world boat drifts here and there, moved by its own inner wind. I am not put out by that.

---

# Verse 7.2

## Sanskrit
मय्यनंतमहांभोधौ
जगद्वीचिः स्वभावतः।
उदेतु वास्तमायातु
न मे वृद्धिर्न च क्षतिः॥

## IAST
mayyanantamahaambhodhau
yagadveechih svabhaavatah.
udetu vaastamaayaatu
na me vriddhirna cha kshatih..

## Translation
Whether the world wave of its own nature rises or disappears in the infinite ocean of myself, I neither gain nor lose anything by that.

---

# Verse 7.3

## Sanskrit
मय्यनंतमहांभोधौ
विश्वं नाम विकल्पना।
अतिशांतो निराकार
एतदेवाहमास्थितः॥

## IAST
mayyanantamahaambhodhau
vishvam naama vikalpanaa.
atishaanto niraakaara
etadevaahamaasthitah..

## Translation
It is in the infinite ocean of myself that the mind-creation called the world takes place. I am supremely peaceful and formless, and I remain as such.

---

# Verse 7.4

## Sanskrit
नात्मा भावेषु नो भावस्-
तत्रानन्ते निरंजने।
इत्यसक्तोऽस्पृहः शान्त एतदेवाहमास्थितः॥

## IAST
naatmaa bhaaveshu no bhaavas-
tatraanante niranjane.
ityasakto'sprihah shaanta etadevaahamaasthitah..

## Translation
My true nature is not contained in objects, nor does any object exist in it, for it is infinite and spotless. So it is unattached, desireless and at peace, and I remain as such.

---

# Verse 7.5

## Sanskrit
अहो चिन्मात्रमेवाहं
इन्द्रजालोपमं जगत्।
अतो मम कथं कुत्र हेयोपादेयकल्पना॥

## IAST
aho chinmaatramevaaham
indrajaalopamam jagat.
ato mama katham kutra heyopaadeyakalpanaa..

## Translation
I am pure consciousness, and the world is like a magician’s show. How could I imagine there is anything there to take up or reject?
