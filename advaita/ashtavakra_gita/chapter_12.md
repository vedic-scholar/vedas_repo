# Verse 12.1

## Sanskrit
कायकृत्यासहः पूर्वं
ततो वाग्विस्तरासहः।
अथ चिन्तासहस्तस्माद् एवमेवाहमास्थितः॥

## IAST
kaayakrityaasahah poorvam
tato vaagvistaraasahah.
atha chintaasahastasmaad evamevaahamaasthitah..

## Translation
Janaka said: First of all I was averse to physical activity, then to lengthy speech, and finally to thought itself, which is why I am now established.

---

# Verse 12.2

## Sanskrit
प्रीत्यभावेन शब्दादेर-
दृश्यत्वेन चात्मनः।
विक्षेपैकाग्रहृदय
एवमेवाहमास्थितः॥

## IAST
preetyabhaavena shabdaadera-
drishyatvena chaatmanah.
vikshepaikaagrahridaya
evamevaahamaasthitah..

## Translation
In the absence of delight in sound and the other senses, and by the fact that I am myself not an object of the senses, my mind is focused and free from distraction — which is why I am now established.

---

# Verse 12.3

## Sanskrit
समाध्यासादिविक्षिप्तौ
व्यवहारः समाधये।
एवं विलोक्य नियमं
एवमेवाहमास्थितः॥

## IAST
samaadhyaasaadivikshiptau
vyavahaarah samaadhaye.
evam vilokya niyamam
evamevaahamaasthitah..

## Translation
Owing to the distraction of such things as wrong identification, one is driven to strive for mental stillness. Recognising this pattern I am now established.

---

# Verse 12.4

## Sanskrit
हेयोपादेयविरहाद्
एवं हर्षविषादयोः।
अभावादद्य हे ब्रह्मन्न्
एवमेवाहमास्थितः॥

## IAST
heyopaadeyavirahaad
evam harshavishaadayoh.
abhaavaadadya he brahmann
evamevaahamaasthitah..

## Translation
By relinquishing the sense of rejection and acceptance, and with pleasure and disappointment ceasing today, brahmin — I am now established.

---

# Verse 12.5

## Sanskrit
आश्रमानाश्रमं ध्यानं
चित्तस्वीकृतवर्जनं।
विकल्पं मम वीक्ष्यै-
तैरेवमेवाहमास्थितः॥

## IAST
aashramaanaashramam dhyaanam
chittasveekritavarjanam.
vikalpam mama veekshyai-
tairevamevaahamaasthitah..

## Translation
Life in a community, then going beyond such a state, meditation and the elimination of mind-made objects — by means of these I have seen my error, and I am now established.

---

# Verse 12.6

## Sanskrit
कर्मानुष्ठानमज्ञानाद्
यथैवोपरमस्तथा।
बुध्वा सम्यगिदं तत्त्वं
एवमेवाहमास्थितः॥

## IAST
karmaanusht'haanamajnyaanaad
yathaivoparamastathaa.
budhvaa samyagidam tattvam
evamevaahamaasthitah..

## Translation
Just as the performance of actions is due to ignorance, so their abandonment is too. By fully recognising this truth, I am now established.

---

# Verse 12.7

## Sanskrit
अचिंत्यं चिंत्यमानोऽपि
चिन्तारूपं भजत्यसौ।
त्यक्त्वा तद्भावनं तस्माद् एवमेवाहमास्थितः॥

## IAST
achintyam chintyamaano'pi
chintaaroopam bhajatyasau.
tyaktvaa tadbhaavanam tasmaad evamevaahamaasthitah..

## Translation
Trying to think the unthinkable, is doing something unnatural to thought. Abandoning such a practice therefore, I am now established.

---

# Verse 12.8

## Sanskrit
एवमेव कृतं येन स
कृतार्थो भवेदसौ।
एवमेव स्वभावो यः
स कृतार्थो भवेदसौ॥

## IAST
evameva kritam yena sa
kritaartho bhavedasau.
evameva svabhaavo yah
sa kritaartho bhavedasau..

## Translation
He who has achieved this has achieved the goal of life. He who is of such a nature has done what has to be done.
