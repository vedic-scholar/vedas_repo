# Verse ।। 1.1.1 ।।

## Sanskrit
ईशा वास्यमिदं सर्वं यत्किञ्च जगत्यां जगत् ।
तेन त्यक्तेन भुञ्जीथा मा गृधः कस्यस्विद्धनम् ॥ १ ॥

## IAST
īśā vāsyamidaṃ sarvaṃ yatkiñca jagatyāṃ jagat |
tena tyaktena bhuñjīthā mā gṛdhaḥ kasyasviddhanam || 1 ||

## Translation
All this, whatever moves in this moving world, is enveloped by God. Therefore, find your enjoyment in renunciation; do not covet what belongs to others.

# Verse ।। 1.1.2 ।।

## Sanskrit
कुर्वन्नेवेह कर्माणि जिजीविषेच्छतं समाः ।
एवं त्वयि नान्यथेतोऽस्ति न कर्म लिप्यते नरे ॥ २ ॥

## IAST
kurvanneveha karmāṇi jijīviṣecchataṃ samāḥ |
evaṃ tvayi nānyatheto'sti na karma lipyate nare || 2 ||

## Translation
Always performing work here, one should wish to live a hundred years. Thus it is for you, not otherwise; actions do not cling to a man.

---

# Verse ।। 1.1.3 ।।

## Sanskrit
असुर्या नाम ते लोका अन्धेन तमसाऽऽवृताः ।
ताँस्ते प्रेत्याभिगच्छन्ति ये के चात्महनो जनाः ॥ ३ ॥

## IAST
asuryā nāma te lokā andhena tamasā'vṛtāḥ |
tā~ste pretyābhigacchanti ye ke cātmahano janāḥ || 3 ||

## Translation
Sunless are those worlds, enveloped in blind darkness, to which all those people go after death who are slayers of their own souls.
