# Verse 1

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
Vāyu, the bright is offered thee, best of the meath at holy rites. Come thou to drink the Soma juice, God, longed-for, on thy team-drawn car.

# Verse 2

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
O Vāyu, thou and Indra are meet drinkers of these Soma-draughts, For unto you the drops proceed as waters gather to the vale.

# Verse 3

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
O Indra-Vāyu, mighty Twain, speeding together, Lords of Strength, Come to our succour with your team, that ye may drink the Soma juice.
