# Verse 1

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
BRING forth your song of praise to mighty Agni, the speedy messenger of earth and heaven, Vaiśvānara, who, with those who wake, hath waxen great in the lap of all the Gods Immortal.

---

# Verse 2

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
Sought in the heavens, on earth is Agni stablished, leader of rivers, Bull of standing waters. Vaiśvānara when he hath grown in glory, shines on the tribes of men with light and treasure.

---

# Verse 3

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
For fear of thee forth fled the dark-hued races, scattered abroad, deserting their possessions, When, glowing, O Vaiśvānara, for Pūru, thou Agni didst light up and rend their castles.

# Verse 4

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
Agni Vaiśvānara, both Earth and Heaven submit them to thy threefold jurisdiction. Refulgent in thine undecaying lustre thou hast invested both the worlds with splendour.

---

# Verse 5

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
Agni, the tawny horses, loudly neighing our resonant hymns that drop with oil, attend thee; Lord of the tribes, our Charioteer of riches, Ensign of days, Vaiśvānara of mornings.

---

# Verse 6

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
In thee, O bright as Mitra, Vasus seated the might of Aduras, for they loved thy spirit. Thou dravest Dasyus from their home, O Agni, and broughtest forth broad light to light the Ārya.

---

# Verse 7

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
Born in the loftiest heaven thou in a moment reachest, like wind, the place where Gods inhabit. Thou, favouring thine offspring, roaredst loudly when giving life to creatures, Jātavedas.

---

# Verse 8

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
Send us that strength, Vaiśvānara, send it, Agni, that strength, O Jātavedas, full of splendour, Wherewith, all-bounteous God, thou pourest riches, as fame wide-spreading, on the man who offers.

---

# Verse 9

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
Agni, bestow upon our chiefs and nobles that famous power, that wealth which feedeth many. Accordant with the Vasus and the Rudras, Agni, Vaiśvānara, give us sure protection.
