# Verse 1

## Sanskrit
अ॒ग्निमी॑ळे पु॒रोहि॑तं य॒ज्ञस्य॑ दे॒वमृ॒त्विज॑म् ।
होता॑रं रत्न॒धात॑मम् ॥ १ ॥

## IAST
agnimīḷe purohitaṃ yajñasya devamṛtvijam |
hotāraṃ ratnadhātamam || 1 ||

## Translation
I praise Agni, the chosen Priest, the God, the Minister of sacrifice, the Hotar, lavisher of wealth.

---

# Verse 2

## Sanskrit
अ॒ग्निः पू॒र्वे॑भि॒रृषि॑भि॒रीड्यो॑ नूत॑नैरु॒त ।
स दे॒वाँ ए॒ह व॑क्षति ॥ २ ॥

## IAST
agniḥ pūrvebhirṛṣibhirīḍyo nūtanairuta |
sa devā~ eha vakṣati || 2 ||

## Translation
Worthy is Agni to be praised by living sages and by sages of old: may he bring the Gods hither.

---

# Verse 3

## Sanskrit
अ॒ग्निना॑ र॒यिम॑श्नव॒त्पोष॑मे​व दि​वे॑दि​वे ।
य॒शसं॑ वी॒रव॑त्तमम् ॥ ३ ॥

## IAST
agninā rayimaśnavatpoṣameva divedive |
yaśasaṃ vīravattamam || 3 ||

## Translation
Through Agni man obtains wealth, yes, plenty day by day, which brings him glory and high heroic lineage.

---

# Verse 4

## Sanskrit
अग्ने॒ यं य॒ज्ञम॑ध्व॒रं वि॒श्वत॑: परि॒भूरसि॑ ।
स इद्दे॒वेषु॑ गच्छति ॥ ४ ॥

## IAST
agne yaṃ yajñamadhvaraṃ viśvataḥ paribhūrasi |
sa iddeveṣu gacchati || 4 ||

## Translation
Agni, the perfect sacrifice which thou encompassest on every side, verily goes to the Gods.
