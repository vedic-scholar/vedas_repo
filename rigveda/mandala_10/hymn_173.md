## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## Translation

# Verse 2

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
Be even here; fall not away be like a mountain unremoved. Stand stedfast here like Indra's self, and hold the kingship in the grasp.

[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
This man hath Indra stablished, made secure by strong oblation's power. May Soma speak a benison, and Brahmaṇaspati, on him.

[Add IAST Transliteration]
