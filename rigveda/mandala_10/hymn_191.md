## Sanskrit

# Verse 2

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
Assemble, speak together: let your minds be all of one accord, As ancient Gods unanimous sit down to their appointed share.
