# Verse 1.1

## Sanskrit
जनक उवाच 
कथं ज्ञानमवाप्नोति,
कथं मुक्तिर्भविष्यति।
वैराग्य च कथं प्राप्तं एतद्
ब्रूहि मम प्रभो॥

## IAST
janaka uvaacha 
katham jnyaanamavaapnoti,
katham muktirbhavishyati.
vairaagya cha katham praaptam etad
broohi mama prabho..

## Translation
Janaka said: How is one to acquire knowledge? How is one to attain liberation? And how is one to reach dispassion? Tell me this, sir.

---

# Verse 1.2

## Sanskrit
अष्टावक्र उवाच 
मुक्तिं इच्छसि चेत्तात,
विषयान विषवत्त्यज।
क्षमार्जवदयातोष, सत्यं
पीयूषवद् भज॥

## IAST
asht'aavakra uvaacha 
muktim ichchhasi chettaata,
vishayaana vishavattyaja.
kshamaarjavadayaatosha, satyam
peeyooshavad bhaja..

## Translation
Ashtavakra said: If you are seeking liberation, my son, avoid the objects of the senses like poison and cultivate tolerance, sincerity, compassion, contentment, and truthfulness as the antidote.

---

# Verse 1.3

## Sanskrit
न पृथ्वी न जलं नाग्निर्न
वायुर्द्यौर्न वा भवान्।
एषां साक्षिणमात्मानं
चिद्रूपं विद्धि मुक्तये॥

## IAST
na prithvee na jalam naagnirna
vaayurdyaurna vaa bhavaan.
eshaam saakshinamaatmaanam
chidroopam viddhi muktaye..

## Translation
You do not consist of any of the elements — earth, water, fire, air, or even ether. To be liberated, know yourself as consisting of consciousness, the witness of these.

---

# Verse 1.4

## Sanskrit
यदि देहं पृथक् कृत्य
चिति विश्राम्य तिष्ठसि।
अधुनैव सुखी शान्तो
बन्धमुक्तो भविष्यसि॥

## IAST
yadi deham prithak kritya
chiti vishraamya tisht'hasi.
adhunaiva sukhee shaanto
bandhamukto bhavishyasi..

## Translation
If only you will remain resting in consciousness, seeing yourself as distinct from the body, then even now you will become happy, peaceful and free from bonds.

---

# Verse 1.5

## Sanskrit
न त्वं विप्रादिको वर्ण:
नाश्रमी नाक्षगोचर:।
असङगोऽसि निराकारो
विश्वसाक्षी सुखी भव॥

## IAST
na tvam vipraadiko varna:
naashramee naakshagochara:.
asangago'si niraakaaro
vishvasaakshee sukhee bhava..

## Translation
You do not belong to the brahmin or any other caste, you are not at any stage, nor are you anything that the eye can see. You are unattached and formless, the witness of everything — so be happy.

---

# Verse 1.6

## Sanskrit
धर्माधर्मौ सुखं दुखं
मानसानि न ते विभो।
न कर्तासि न भोक्तासि
मुक्त एवासि सर्वदा॥

## IAST
dharmaadharmau sukham dukham
maanasaani na te vibho.
na kartaasi na bhoktaasi
mukta evaasi sarvadaa..

## Translation
Righteousness and unrighteousness, pleasure and pain are purely of the mind and are no concern of yours. You are neither the doer nor the reaper of the consequences, so you are always free.

---

# Verse 1.7

## Sanskrit
एको द्रष्टासि सर्वस्य
मुक्तप्रायोऽसि सर्वदा।
अयमेव हि ते बन्धो
द्रष्टारं पश्यसीतरम्॥

## IAST
eko drasht'aasi sarvasya
muktapraayo'si sarvadaa.
ayameva hi te bandho
drasht'aaram pashyaseetaram..

## Translation
You are the one witness of everything and are always completely free. The cause of your bondage is that you see the witness as something other than this.

---

# Verse 1.8

## Sanskrit
अहं कर्तेत्यहंमान
महाकृष्णाहिदंशितः।
नाहं कर्तेति विश्वासामृतं
पीत्वा सुखं भव॥

## IAST
aham kartetyahammaana
mahaakrishnaahidamshitah.
naaham karteti vishvaasaamritam
peetvaa sukham bhava..

## Translation
Since you have been bitten by the black snake, the opinion about yourself that “I am the doer,” drink the antidote of faith in the fact that “I am not the doer,” and be happy.

---

# Verse 1.9

## Sanskrit
एको विशुद्धबोधोऽहं
इति निश्चयवह्निना।
प्रज्वाल्याज्ञानगहनं
वीतशोकः सुखी भव॥

## IAST
eko vishuddhabodho'ham
iti nishchayavahninaa.
prajvaalyaajnyaanagahanam
veetashokah sukhee bhava..

## Translation
Burn down the forest of ignorance with the fire of the understanding that “I am the one pure awareness,” and be happy and free from distress.

---

# Verse 1.10

## Sanskrit
यत्र विश्वमिदं भाति
कल्पितं रज्जुसर्पवत्।
आनंदपरमानन्दः स
बोधस्त्वं सुखं चर॥

## IAST
yatra vishvamidam bhaati
kalpitam rajjusarpavat.
aanandaparamaanandah sa
bodhastvam sukham chara..

## Translation
That in which all this — imagined like the snake in a rope — appears: that joy, supreme joy, and awareness is what you are, so be happy.

---

# Verse 1.11

## Sanskrit
मुक्ताभिमानी मुक्तो हि
बद्धो बद्धाभिमान्यपि।
किवदन्तीह सत्येयं
या मतिः सा गतिर्भवेत्॥

## IAST
muktaabhimaanee mukto hi
baddho baddhaabhimaanyapi.
kivadanteeha satyeyam
yaa matih saa gatirbhavet..

## Translation
If one thinks of oneself as free, one is free, and if one thinks of oneself as bound, one is bound. Here this saying is true, “Thinking makes it so.”

---

# Verse 1.12

## Sanskrit
आत्मा साक्षी विभुः
पूर्ण एको मुक्तश्चिदक्रियः।
असंगो निःस्पृहः शान्तो
भ्रमात्संसारवानिव॥

## IAST
aatmaa saakshee vibhuh
poorna eko muktashchidakriyah.
asango nihsprihah shaanto
bhramaatsamsaaravaaniva..

## Translation
Your real nature is as the one perfect, free, and actionless consciousness, the all-pervading witness — unattached to anything, desireless and at peace. It is from illusion that you seem to be involved in samsara.

---

# Verse 1.13

## Sanskrit
कूटस्थं बोधमद्वैत-
मात्मानं परिभावय।
आभासोऽहं भ्रमं मुक्त्वा
भावं बाह्यमथान्तरम्॥

## IAST
koot'astham bodhamadvaita-
maatmaanam paribhaavaya.
aabhaaso'ham bhramam muktvaa
bhaavam baahyamathaantaram..

## Translation
Meditate on yourself as motionless awareness, free from any dualism, giving up the mistaken idea that you are just a derivative consciousness or anything external or internal.

---

# Verse 1.14

## Sanskrit
देहाभिमानपाशेन चिरं
बद्धोऽसि पुत्रक।
बोधोऽहं ज्ञानखंगेन
तन्निष्कृत्य सुखी भव॥

## IAST
dehaabhimaanapaashena chiram
baddho'si putraka.
bodho'ham jnyaanakhangena
tannishkritya sukhee bhava..

## Translation
You have long been trapped in the snare of identification with the body. Sever it with the knife of knowledge that “I am awareness,” and be happy, my son.

---

# Verse 1.15

## Sanskrit
निःसंगो निष्क्रियोऽसि
त्वं स्वप्रकाशो निरंजनः।
अयमेव हि ते बन्धः
समाधिमनुतिष्ठति॥

## IAST
nihsango nishkriyo'si
tvam svaprakaasho niranjanah.
ayameva hi te bandhah
samaadhimanutisht'hati..

## Translation
You are really unbound and actionless, self-illuminating and spotless already. The cause of your bondage is that you are still resorting to stilling the mind.

---

# Verse 1.16

## Sanskrit
त्वया व्याप्तमिदं विश्वं
त्वयि प्रोतं यथार्थतः।
शुद्धबुद्धस्वरुपस्त्वं मा
गमः क्षुद्रचित्तताम्॥

## IAST
tvayaa vyaaptamidam vishvam
tvayi protam yathaarthatah.
shuddhabuddhasvarupastvam maa
gamah kshudrachittataam..

## Translation
All of this is really filled by you and strung out in you, for what you consist of is pure awareness — so don’t be small-minded.

---

# Verse 1.17

## Sanskrit
निरपेक्षो निर्विकारो
निर्भरः शीतलाशयः।
अगाधबुद्धिरक्षुब्धो भव
चिन्मात्रवासन:॥

## IAST
nirapeksho nirvikaaro
nirbharah sheetalaashayah.
agaadhabuddhirakshubdho bhava
chinmaatravaasana:..

## Translation
You are unconditioned and changeless, formless and immovable, unfathomable awareness, unperturbable: so hold to nothing but consciousness.

---

# Verse 1.18

## Sanskrit
साकारमनृतं विद्धि
निराकारं तु निश्चलं।
एतत्तत्त्वोपदेशेन
न पुनर्भवसंभव:॥

## IAST
saakaaramanritam viddhi
niraakaaram tu nishchalam.
etattattvopadeshena
na punarbhavasambhava:..

## Translation
Recognise that the apparent is unreal, while the unmanifest is abiding. Through this initiation into truth you will escape falling into unreality again.

---

# Verse 1.19

## Sanskrit
यथैवादर्शमध्यस्थे
रूपेऽन्तः परितस्तु सः।
तथैवाऽस्मिन् शरीरेऽन्तः
परितः परमेश्वरः॥

## IAST
yathaivaadarshamadhyasthe
roope'ntah paritastu sah.
tathaivaa'smin shareere'ntah
paritah parameshvarah..

## Translation
Just as a mirror exists everywhere both within and apart from its reflected images, so the Supreme Lord exists everywhere within and apart from this body.

---

# Verse 1.20

## Sanskrit
एकं सर्वगतं व्योम
बहिरन्तर्यथा घटे।
नित्यं निरन्तरं ब्रह्म
सर्वभूतगणे तथा॥

## IAST
ekam sarvagatam vyoma
bahirantaryathaa ghat'e.
nityam nirantaram brahma
sarvabhootagane tathaa..

## Translation
Just as one and the same all-pervading space exists within and without a jar, so the eternal, everlasting God exists in the totality of things.
