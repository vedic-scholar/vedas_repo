subtitle: "Natural Dissolution of the Mind"
synopsis: "Janaka states that none can comprehend the ways of a jnani who roams the world with perfect freedom with content less (devoid of thoughts) mind as his very nature and therefore desire less and anchored firmly in the awareness of 'Being'."
---

# Verse 14.1

## Sanskrit
प्रकृत्या शून्यचित्तो यः
प्रमादाद् भावभावनः।
निद्रितो बोधित इव क्षीण-
संस्मरणो हि सः॥

## IAST
prakrityaa shoonyachitto yah
pramaadaad bhaavabhaavanah.
nidrito bodhita iva ksheena-
samsmarano hi sah..

## Translation
Janaka said: He who by nature is empty-minded, and who thinks of things only unintentionally, is freed from deliberate remembering like one awakened from a dream.

# Verse 14.2

## Sanskrit
क्व धनानि क्व मित्राणि
क्व मे विषयदस्यवः।
क्व शास्त्रं क्व च विज्ञानं
यदा मे गलिता स्पृहा॥

## IAST
kva dhanaani kva mitraani
kva me vishayadasyavah.
kva shaastram kva cha vijnyaanam
yadaa me galitaa sprihaa..

## Translation
When my desire has been eliminated, I have no wealth, friends, robbers, senses, scriptures or knowledge.

---

# Verse 14.3

## Sanskrit
विज्ञाते साक्षिपुरुषे
परमात्मनि चेश्वरे।
नैराश्ये बंधमोक्षे च
न चिंता मुक्तये मम॥

## IAST
vijnyaate saakshipurushe
paramaatmani cheshvare.
nairaashye bandhamokshe cha
na chintaa muktaye mama..

## Translation
Realising my supreme self-nature in the Person of the Witness, the Lord, and the state of desirelessness in bondage or liberation, I feel no inclination for liberation.

---

# Verse 14.4

## Sanskrit
अंतर्विकल्पशून्यस्य
बहिः स्वच्छन्दचारिणः।
भ्रान्तस्येव दशास्तास्तास्-
तादृशा एव जानते॥

## IAST
antarvikalpashoonyasya
bahih svachchhandachaarinah.
bhraantasyeva dashaastaastaas-
taadrishaa eva jaanate..

## Translation
The various states of one who is free of uncertainty within, and who outwardly wanders about as he pleases like an idiot, can only be known by someone in the same condition.
