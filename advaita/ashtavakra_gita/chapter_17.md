# Verse 17.1

## Sanskrit
अष्टावक्र उवाच 
तेन ज्ञानफलं प्राप्तं
योगाभ्यासफलं तथा।
तृप्तः स्वच्छेन्द्रियो नित्यं
एकाकी रमते तु यः॥

## IAST
asht'aavakra uvaacha 
tena jnyaanaphalam praaptam
yogaabhyaasaphalam tathaa.
triptah svachchhendriyo nityam
ekaakee ramate tu yah..

## Translation
Ashtavakra said: He who is content, with purified senses, and always enjoys solitude, has gained the fruit of knowledge and the fruit of the practice of yoga too.

---

# Verse 17.2

## Sanskrit
न कदाचिज्जगत्यस्मिन्
तत्त्वज्ञा हन्त खिद्यति।
यत एकेन तेनेदं
पूर्णं ब्रह्माण्डमण्डलम्॥

## IAST
na kadaachijjagatyasmin
tattvajnyaa hanta khidyati.
yata ekena tenedam
poornam brahmaand'amand'alam..

## Translation
The knower of truth is never distressed in this world, for the whole round world is full of himself alone.

---

# Verse 17.3

## Sanskrit
न जातु विषयाः केऽपि
स्वारामं हर्षयन्त्यमी।
सल्लकीपल्लवप्रीत-
मिवेभं निंबपल्लवाः॥

## IAST
na jaatu vishayaah ke'pi
svaaraamam harshayantyamee.
sallakeepallavapreeta-
mivebham nimbapallavaah..

## Translation
None of these senses please a man who has found satisfaction within, just as Nimba leaves do not please the elephant that has acquired the taste for Sallaki leaves.

---

# Verse 17.4

## Sanskrit
यस्तु भोगेषु भुक्तेषु
न भवत्यधिवासिता।
अभुक्तेषु निराकांक्षी
तदृशो भवदुर्लभः॥

## IAST
yastu bhogeshu bhukteshu
na bhavatyadhivaasitaa.
abhukteshu niraakaankshee
tadrisho bhavadurlabhah..

## Translation
The man is rare who is not attached to the things he has enjoyed, and does not hanker after the things he has not enjoyed.

---

# Verse 17.5

## Sanskrit
बुभुक्षुरिह संसारे
मुमुक्षुरपि दृश्यते।
भोगमोक्षनिराकांक्षी
विरलो हि महाशयः॥

## IAST
bubhukshuriha samsaare
mumukshurapi drishyate.
bhogamokshaniraakaankshee
viralo hi mahaashayah..

## Translation
Those who desire pleasure and those who desire liberation are both found in samsara, but the great-souled man who desires neither pleasure nor liberation is rare indeed.

---

# Verse 17.6

## Sanskrit
धर्मार्थकाममोक्षेषु
जीविते मरणे तथा।
कस्याप्युदारचित्तस्य
हेयोपादेयता न हि॥

## IAST
dharmaarthakaamamoksheshu
yeevite marane tathaa.
kasyaapyudaarachittasya
heyopaadeyataa na hi..

## Translation
It is only the noble-minded who is free from attraction or repulsion to religion, wealth, sensuality, and life and death too.

---

# Verse 17.7

## Sanskrit
वांछा न विश्वविलये
न द्वेषस्तस्य च स्थितौ।
यथा जीविकया तस्माद्
धन्य आस्ते यथा सुखम्॥

## IAST
vaanchhaa na vishvavilaye
na dveshastasya cha sthitau.
yathaa jeevikayaa tasmaad
dhanya aaste yathaa sukham..

## Translation
He feels no desire for the elimination of all this, nor anger at its continuing, so the fortunate man lives happily with whatever sustinence presents itself.

---

# Verse 17.8

## Sanskrit
कृतार्थोऽनेन ज्ञानेने-
त्येवं गलितधीः कृती।
पश्यन् शृण्वन् स्पृशन् जिघ्रन्न्
अश्नन्नस्ते यथा सुखम्॥

## IAST
kritaartho'nena jnyaanene-
tyevam galitadheeh kritee.
pashyan shrinvan sprishan jighrann
ashnannaste yathaa sukham..

## Translation
Thus fulfilled through this knowledge, contented, and with the thinking mind emptied, he lives happily just seeing, hearing, feeling, smelling, and tasting.

---

# Verse 17.9

## Sanskrit
शून्या दृष्टिर्वृथा चेष्टा
विकलानीन्द्रियाणि च।
न स्पृहा न विरक्तिर्वा
क्षीणसंसारसागरे॥

## IAST
shoonyaa drisht'irvrithaa chesht'aa
vikalaaneendriyaani cha.
na sprihaa na viraktirvaa
ksheenasamsaarasaagare..

## Translation
In him for whom the ocean of samsara has dried up, there is neither attachment or aversion. His gaze is vacant, his behaviour purposeless, and his senses inactive.

---

# Verse 17.10

## Sanskrit
न जगर्ति न निद्राति
नोन्मीलति न मीलति।
अहो परदशा क्वापि
वर्तते मुक्तचेतसः॥

## IAST
na jagarti na nidraati
nonmeelati na meelati.
aho paradashaa kvaapi
vartate muktachetasah..

## Translation
Surely the supreme state is everywhere for the liberated mind. He is neither awake nor asleep, and neither opens nor closes his eyes.

---

# Verse 17.11

## Sanskrit
सर्वत्र दृश्यते स्वस्थः
सर्वत्र विमलाशयः।
समस्तवासना मुक्तो
मुक्तः सर्वत्र राजते॥

## IAST
sarvatra drishyate svasthah
sarvatra vimalaashayah.
samastavaasanaa mukto
muktah sarvatra raajate..

## Translation
The liberated man is resplendent everywhere, free from all desires. Everywhere he appears self-possessed and pure of heart.

---

# Verse 17.12

## Sanskrit
पश्यन् शृण्वन् स्पृशन् जिघ्रन्न्
अश्नन् गृण्हन् वदन् व्रजन्।
ईहितानीहितैर्मुक्तो
मुक्त एव महाशयः॥

## IAST
pashyan shrinvan sprishan jighrann
ashnan grinhan vadan vrajan.
eehitaaneehitairmukto
mukta eva mahaashayah..

## Translation
Seeing, hearing, feeling, smelling, tasting, speaking, and walking about, the great-souled man who is freed from trying to achieve or avoid anything is free indeed.

---

# Verse 17.13

## Sanskrit
न निन्दति न च स्तौति
न हृष्यति न कुप्यति।
न ददाति न गृण्हाति
मुक्तः सर्वत्र नीरसः॥

## IAST
na nindati na cha stauti
na hrishyati na kupyati.
na dadaati na grinhaati
muktah sarvatra neerasah..

## Translation
The liberated man is free from desires everywhere. He neither blames, praises, rejoices, is disappointed, gives, nor takes.

---

# Verse 17.14

## Sanskrit
सानुरागां स्त्रियं दृष्ट्वा
मृत्युं वा समुपस्थितं।
अविह्वलमनाः स्वस्थो
मुक्त एव महाशयः॥

## IAST
saanuraagaam striyam drisht'vaa
mrityum vaa samupasthitam.
avihvalamanaah svastho
mukta eva mahaashayah..

## Translation
When a great-souled one is unperturbed in mind, and equally self-possessed at either the sight of a woman inflamed with desire or at approaching death, he is truly liberated.

---

# Verse 17.15

## Sanskrit
सुखे दुःखे नरे नार्यां
संपत्सु विपत्सु च।
विशेषो नैव धीरस्य
सर्वत्र समदर्शिनः॥

## IAST
sukhe duhkhe nare naaryaam
sampatsu vipatsu cha.
vishesho naiva dheerasya
sarvatra samadarshinah..

## Translation
There is no distinction between pleasure and pain, man and woman, success and failure for the wise man who looks on everything as equal.

---

# Verse 17.16

## Sanskrit
न हिंसा नैव कारुण्यं
नौद्धत्यं न च दीनता।
नाश्चर्यं नैव च क्षोभः
क्षीणसंसरणे नरे॥

## IAST
na himsaa naiva kaarunyam
nauddhatyam na cha deenataa.
naashcharyam naiva cha kshobhah
ksheenasamsarane nare..

## Translation
There is no aggression nor compassion, no pride nor humility, no wonder nor confusion for the man whose days of samsara are over.

---

# Verse 17.17

## Sanskrit
न मुक्तो विषयद्वेष्टा
न वा विषयलोलुपः।
असंसक्तमना नित्यं
प्राप्ताप्राप्तमुपाश्नुते॥

## IAST
na mukto vishayadvesht'aa
na vaa vishayalolupah.
asamsaktamanaa nityam
praaptaapraaptamupaashnute..

## Translation
The liberated man is not averse to the senses nor is he attached to them. He enjoys hinself continually with an unattached mind in both success and failure.

---

# Verse 17.18

## Sanskrit
समाधानसमाधान-
हिताहितविकल्पनाः।
शून्यचित्तो न जानाति
कैवल्यमिव संस्थितः॥

## IAST
samaadhaanasamaadhaana-
hitaahitavikalpanaah.
shoonyachitto na jaanaati
kaivalyamiva samsthitah..

## Translation
One established in the Absolute state with an empty mind does not know the alternatives of inner stillness and lack of inner stillness, and of good and evil.

---

# Verse 17.19

## Sanskrit
निर्ममो निरहंकारो
न किंचिदिति निश्चितः।
अन्तर्गलितसर्वाशः
कुर्वन्नपि करोति न॥

## IAST
nirmamo nirahankaaro
na kinchiditi nishchitah.
antargalitasarvaashah
kurvannapi karoti na..

## Translation
A man free of “me” and “mine” and of a sense of responsibility, aware that “Nothing exists,” with all desires extinguished within, does not act even in acting.

---

# Verse 17.20

## Sanskrit
मनःप्रकाशसंमोह
स्वप्नजाड्यविवर्जितः।
दशां कामपि संप्राप्तो
भवेद् गलितमानसः॥

## IAST
manahprakaashasammoha
svapnajaad'yavivarjitah.
dashaam kaamapi sampraapto
bhaved galitamaanasah..

## Translation
He whose thinking mind is dissolved achieves the indescribable state and is free from the mental display of delusion, dream, and ignorance.
