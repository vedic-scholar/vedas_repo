# Verse 5.1

## Sanskrit
अष्टावक्र उवाच 
न ते संगोऽस्ति केनापि
किं शुद्धस्त्यक्तुमिच्छसि।
संघातविलयं कुर्वन्-
नेवमेव लयं व्रज॥

## IAST
asht'aavakra uvaacha 
na te sango'sti kenaapi
kim shuddhastyaktumichchhasi.
sanghaatavilayam kurvan-
nevameva layam vraja..

## Translation
Ashtavakra said: You are not bound by anything. What does a pure person like you need to renounce? Putting the complex organism to rest, you can find peace.

# Verse 5.2

## Sanskrit
उदेति भवतो विश्वं
वारिधेरिव बुद्बुदः।
इति ज्ञात्वैकमात्मानं
एवमेव लयं व्रज॥

## IAST
udeti bhavato vishvam
vaaridheriva budbudah.
iti jnyaatvaikamaatmaanam
evameva layam vraja..

## Translation
All this arises out of you, like a bubble out of the sea. Knowing yourself like this to be but one, you can find peace.

---

# Verse 5.3

## Sanskrit
प्रत्यक्षमप्यवस्तुत्वाद्
विश्वं नास्त्यमले त्वयि।
रज्जुसर्प इव व्यक्तं
एवमेव लयं व्रज॥

## IAST
pratyakshamapyavastutvaad
vishvam naastyamale tvayi.
rajjusarpa iva vyaktam
evameva layam vraja..

## Translation
In spite of being in front of your eyes, all this, being insubstantial, does not exist in you, spotless as you are. It is an appearance like the snake in a rope, so you can find peace.

---

# Verse 5.4

## Sanskrit
समदुःखसुखः पूर्ण
आशानैराश्ययोः समः।
समजीवितमृत्युः सन्-
नेवमेव लयं व्रज॥

## IAST
samaduhkhasukhah poorna
aashaanairaashyayoh samah.
samajeevitamrityuh san-
nevameva layam vraja..

## Translation
Equal in pain and in pleasure, equal in hope and in disappointment, equal in life and in death, and complete as you are, you can find peace.
