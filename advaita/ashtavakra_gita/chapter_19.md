category: "Advaita Vedanta"
chapter: "Chapter 19: Svamahima"
subtitle: "Majesty of the Self"
synopsis: "Janaka affirms his firm abidance in the glory of the self; by his non-mediated experience of jnana gained by hamstring all thoughts - positive and negative. He further declares that except the glory of the self, he has nothing else."
---

# Verse 19.1

## Sanskrit
जनक उवाच-
तत्त्वविज्ञानसन्दंश-
मादाय हृदयोदरात्।
नानाविधपरामर्श-
शल्योद्धारः कृतो मया॥

## IAST
janaka uvaacha-
tattvavijnyaanasandamsha-
maadaaya hridayodaraat.
naanaavidhaparaamarsha-
shalyoddhaarah krito mayaa..

## Translation
Janaka said: Using the tweezers of the knowledge of the truth I have managed to extract the painful thorn of endless opinions from the recesses of my heart.

---

# Verse 19.2

## Sanskrit
क्व धर्मः क्व च वा कामः
क्व चार्थः क्व विवेकिता।
क्व द्वैतं क्व च वाऽद्वैतं
स्वमहिम्नि स्थितस्य मे॥

## IAST
kva dharmah kva cha vaa kaamah
kva chaarthah kva vivekitaa.
kva dvaitam kva cha vaa'dvaitam
svamahimni sthitasya me..

## Translation
For me, established in my own glory, there are no religious obligations, sensuality, possessions, philosophy, duality, or even nonduality.

---

# Verse 19.3

## Sanskrit
क्व भूतं क्व भविष्यद् वा
वर्तमानमपि क्व वा।
क्व देशः क्व च वा नित्यं
स्वमहिम्नि स्थितस्य मे॥

## IAST
kva bhootam kva bhavishyad vaa
vartamaanamapi kva vaa.
kva deshah kva cha vaa nityam
svamahimni sthitasya me..

## Translation
For me established in my own glory, there is no past, future, or present. There is no space or even eternity.

---

# Verse 19.4

## Sanskrit
क्व चात्मा क्व च वानात्मा
क्व शुभं क्वाशुभं तथा।
क्व चिन्ता क्व च वाचिन्ता
स्वमहिम्नि स्थितस्य मे॥

## IAST
kva chaatmaa kva cha vaanaatmaa
kva shubham kvaashubham tathaa.
kva chintaa kva cha vaachintaa
svamahimni sthitasya me..

## Translation
For me established in my own glory, there is no self or non-self, no good or evil, no thought or even absence of thought.

---

# Verse 19.5

## Sanskrit
क्व स्वप्नः क्व सुषुप्तिर्वा
क्व च जागरणं तथा।
क्व तुरियं भयं वापि
स्वमहिम्नि स्थितस्य मे॥

## IAST
kva svapnah kva sushuptirvaa
kva cha jaagaranam tathaa.
kva turiyam bhayam vaapi
svamahimni sthitasya me..

## Translation
For me established in my own glory, there is no dreaming or deep sleep, no waking nor fourth state beyond them, and certainly no fear.

---

# Verse 19.6

## Sanskrit
क्व दूरं क्व समीपं वा बाह्यं
क्वाभ्यन्तरं क्व वा।
क्व स्थूलं क्व च वा सूक्ष्मं
स्वमहिम्नि स्थितस्य मे॥

## IAST
kva dooram kva sameepam vaa baahyam
kvaabhyantaram kva vaa.
kva sthoolam kva cha vaa sookshmam
svamahimni sthitasya me..

## Translation
For me established in my own glory, there is nothing far away and nothing near, nothing within or without, nothing large and nothing small.

---

# Verse 19.7

## Sanskrit
क्व मृत्युर्जीवितं वा क्व लोकाः
क्वास्य क्व लौकिकं।
क्व लयः क्व समाधिर्वा
स्वमहिम्नि स्थितस्य मे॥

## IAST
kva mrityurjeevitam vaa kva lokaah
kvaasya kva laukikam.
kva layah kva samaadhirvaa
svamahimni sthitasya me..

## Translation
For me established in my own glory, there is no life or death, no worlds or things of this world, no distraction and no stillness of mind.

---

# Verse 19.8

## Sanskrit
अलं त्रिवर्गकथया
योगस्य कथयाप्यलं।
अलं विज्ञानकथया
विश्रान्तस्य ममात्मनि॥

## IAST
alam trivargakathayaa
yogasya kathayaapyalam.
alam vijnyaanakathayaa
vishraantasya mamaatmani..

## Translation
For me remaining in myself, there is no need for talk of the three goals of life, of yoga or of knowledge.
