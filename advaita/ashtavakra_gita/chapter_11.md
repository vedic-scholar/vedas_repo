# Verse 11.1

## Sanskrit
अष्टावक्र उवाच 
भावाभावविकारश्च
स्वभावादिति निश्चयी।
निर्विकारो गतक्लेशः
सुखेनैवोपशाम्यति॥

## IAST
asht'aavakra uvaacha 
bhaavaabhaavavikaarashcha
svabhaavaaditi nishchayee.
nirvikaaro gatakleshah
sukhenaivopashaamyati..

## Translation
Ashtavakra said: Unmoved and undistressed, realising that being, non-being and change are of the very nature of things, one easily finds peace.

---

# Verse 11.2

## Sanskrit
ईश्वरः सर्वनिर्माता
नेहान्य इति निश्चयी।
अन्तर्गलितसर्वाशः शान्तः
क्वापि न सज्जते॥

## IAST
eeshvarah sarvanirmaataa
nehaanya iti nishchayee.
antargalitasarvaashah shaantah
kvaapi na sajjate..

## Translation
At peace, having shed all desires within, and realising that nothing exists here but the Lord, the Creator of all things, one is no longer attached to anything.

---

# Verse 11.3

## Sanskrit
आपदः संपदः काले
दैवादेवेति निश्चयी।
तृप्तः स्वस्थेन्द्रियो नित्यं
न वान्छति न शोचति॥

## IAST
aapadah sampadah kaale
daivaadeveti nishchayee.
triptah svasthendriyo nityam
na vaanchhati na shochati..

## Translation
Realising that misfortune and fortune come in their own time from fortune, one is contented, one’s senses under control, and does not like or dislike.

---

# Verse 11.4

## Sanskrit
सुखदुःखे जन्ममृत्यू
दैवादेवेति निश्चयी।
साध्यादर्शी निरायासः
कुर्वन्नपि न लिप्यते॥

## IAST
sukhaduhkhe janmamrityoo
daivaadeveti nishchayee.
saadhyaadarshee niraayaasah
kurvannapi na lipyate..

## Translation
Realising that pleasure and pain, birth and death are from destiny, and that one’s desires cannot be achieved, one remains inactive, and even when acting does not get attached.

---

# Verse 11.5

## Sanskrit
चिन्तया जायते दुःखं
नान्यथेहेति निश्चयी।
तया हीनः सुखी शान्तः
सर्वत्र गलितस्पृहः॥

## IAST
chintayaa jaayate duhkham
naanyatheheti nishchayee.
tayaa heenah sukhee shaantah
sarvatra galitasprihah..

## Translation
Realising that suffering arises from nothing other than thought, dropping all desires one rids oneself of it, and is happy and at peace everywhere.

---

# Verse 11.6

## Sanskrit
नाहं देहो न मे देहो
बोधोऽहमिति निश्चयी।
कैवल्यं इव संप्राप्तो
न स्मरत्यकृतं कृतम्॥

## IAST
naaham deho na me deho
bodho'hamiti nishchayee.
kaivalyam iva sampraapto
na smaratyakritam kritam..

## Translation
Realising, “I am not the body, nor is the body mine. I am awareness,” one attains the supreme state and no longer remembers things done or undone.

---

# Verse 11.7

## Sanskrit
आब्रह्मस्तंबपर्यन्तं
अहमेवेति निश्चयी।
निर्विकल्पः शुचिः शान्तः प्राप्ताप्राप्तविनिर्वृतः॥

## IAST
aabrahmastambaparyantam
ahameveti nishchayee.
nirvikalpah shuchih shaantah praaptaapraaptavinirvritah..

## Translation
Realising, “I alone exist, from Brahma down to the last clump of grass,” one becomes free from uncertainty, pure, at peace, and unconcerned about what has been attained or not.

---

# Verse 11.8

## Sanskrit
नाश्चर्यमिदं विश्वं
न किंचिदिति निश्चयी।
निर्वासनः स्फूर्तिमात्रो
न किंचिदिव शाम्यति॥

## IAST
naashcharyamidam vishvam
na kinchiditi nishchayee.
nirvaasanah sphoortimaatro
na kinchidiva shaamyati..

## Translation
Realising that all this varied and wonderful world is nothing, one becomes pure receptivity, free from inclinations, and as if nothing existed, one finds peace.
