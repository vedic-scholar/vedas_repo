subtitle: "Transcendence of the Self"
synopsis: "Janaka conclusively affirms that in them who is pure awareness personified there exist neither the instruments of action, nor of perception, none whatsoever of the dualities, the triads of actions, the world, the jivas; maya, samsara and mukti."
---

# Verse 20.1

## Sanskrit
क्व भूतानि क्व देहो वा
क्वेन्द्रियाणि क्व वा मनः।
क्व शून्यं क्व च नैराश्यं
मत्स्वरूपे निरंजने॥

## IAST
kva bhootaani kva deho vaa
kvendriyaani kva vaa manah.
kva shoonyam kva cha nairaashyam
matsvaroope niranjane..

## Translation
Janaka said: In my unblemished nature there are no elements, no body, no faculties, no mind. There is no void and no despair.

---

# Verse 20.2

## Sanskrit
क्व शास्त्रं क्वात्मविज्ञानं
क्व वा निर्विषयं मनः।
क्व तृप्तिः क्व वितृष्णत्वं
गतद्वन्द्वस्य मे सदा॥

## IAST
kva shaastram kvaatmavijnyaanam
kva vaa nirvishayam manah.
kva triptih kva vitrishnatvam
gatadvandvasya me sadaa..

## Translation
For me, free from the sense of dualism, there are no scriptures, no self-knowledge, no mind free from an object, no satisfaction and no freedom from desire.

---

# Verse 20.3

## Sanskrit
क्व विद्या क्व च वाविद्या
क्वाहं क्वेदं मम क्व वा।
क्व बन्ध क्व च वा मोक्षः
स्वरूपस्य क्व रूपिता॥

## IAST
kva vidyaa kva cha vaavidyaa
kvaaham kvedam mama kva vaa.
kva bandha kva cha vaa mokshah
svaroopasya kva roopitaa..

## Translation
There is no knowledge or ignorance, no “me,” “this,” or “mine,” no bondage, no liberation, and no property of self-nature.

---

# Verse 20.4

## Sanskrit
क्व प्रारब्धानि कर्माणि
जीवन्मुक्तिरपि क्व वा।
क्व तद् विदेहकैवल्यं
निर्विशेषस्य सर्वदा॥

## IAST
kva praarabdhaani karmaani
yeevanmuktirapi kva vaa.
kva tad videhakaivalyam
nirvisheshasya sarvadaa..

## Translation
For him who is always free from individual characteristics there is no antecedent causal action, no liberation during life, and no fulfilment at death.

---

# Verse 20.5

## Sanskrit
क्व कर्ता क्व च वा भोक्ता
निष्क्रियं स्फुरणं क्व वा।
क्वापरोक्षं फलं वा क्व
निःस्वभावस्य मे सदा॥

## IAST
kva kartaa kva cha vaa bhoktaa
nishkriyam sphuranam kva vaa.
kvaaparoksham phalam vaa kva
nihsvabhaavasya me sadaa..

## Translation
For me, free from individuality, there is no doer and no reaper of the consequences, no cessation of action, no arising of thought, no immediate object, and no idea of results.

---

# Verse 20.6

## Sanskrit
क्व लोकं क्व मुमुक्षुर्वा
क्व योगी ज्ञानवान् क्व वा।
क्व बद्धः क्व च वा मुक्तः
स्वस्वरूपेऽहमद्वये॥

## IAST
kva lokam kva mumukshurvaa
kva yogee jnyaanavaan kva vaa.
kva baddhah kva cha vaa muktah
svasvaroope'hamadvaye..

## Translation
There is no world, no seeker for liberation, no yogi, no seer, no one bound and no one liberated. I remain in my own nondual nature.

---

# Verse 20.7

## Sanskrit
क्व सृष्टिः क्व च संहारः
क्व साध्यं क्व च साधनं।
क्व साधकः क्व सिद्धिर्वा
स्वस्वरूपेऽहमद्वये॥

## IAST
kva srisht'ih kva cha samhaarah
kva saadhyam kva cha saadhanam.
kva saadhakah kva siddhirvaa
svasvaroope'hamadvaye..

## Translation
There is no emanation or return, no goal, means, seeker or achievement. I remain in my own nondual nature.

---

# Verse 20.8

## Sanskrit
क्व प्रमाता प्रमाणं वा
क्व प्रमेयं क्व च प्रमा।
क्व किंचित् क्व न किंचिद्
वा सर्वदा विमलस्य मे॥

## IAST
kva pramaataa pramaanam vaa
kva prameyam kva cha pramaa.
kva kinchit kva na kinchid
vaa sarvadaa vimalasya me..

## Translation
For me who am forever unblemished, there is no assessor, no standard, nothing to assess, and no assessment.

---

# Verse 20.9

## Sanskrit
क्व विक्षेपः क्व चैकाग्र्यं
क्व निर्बोधः क्व मूढता।
क्व हर्षः क्व विषादो वा
सर्वदा निष्क्रियस्य मे॥

## IAST
kva vikshepah kva chaikaagryam
kva nirbodhah kva mood'hataa.
kva harshah kva vishaado vaa
sarvadaa nishkriyasya me..

## Translation
For me who am forever actionless, there is no distraction or one-pointedness of mind, no lack of understanding, no stupidity, no joy and no sorrow.

---

# Verse 20.10

## Sanskrit
क्व चैष व्यवहारो वा
क्व च सा परमार्थता।
क्व सुखं क्व च वा दुखं
निर्विमर्शस्य मे सदा॥

## IAST
kva chaisha vyavahaaro vaa
kva cha saa paramaarthataa.
kva sukham kva cha vaa dukham
nirvimarshasya me sadaa..

## Translation
For me who am always free from deliberations there is neither conventional truth nor absolute truth, no happiness and no suffering.

---

# Verse 20.11

## Sanskrit
क्व माया क्व च संसारः
क्व प्रीतिर्विरतिः क्व वा।
क्व जीवः क्व च तद्ब्रह्म
सर्वदा विमलस्य मे॥

## IAST
kva maayaa kva cha samsaarah
kva preetirviratih kva vaa.
kva jeevah kva cha tadbrahma
sarvadaa vimalasya me..

## Translation
For me who am forever pure there is no illusion, no samsara, no attachment or detachment, no living organism, and no God.

---

# Verse 20.12

## Sanskrit
क्व प्रवृत्तिर्निर्वृत्तिर्वा
क्व मुक्तिः क्व च बन्धनं।
कूटस्थनिर्विभागस्य
स्वस्थस्य मम सर्वदा॥

## IAST
kva pravrittirnirvrittirvaa
kva muktih kva cha bandhanam.
koot'asthanirvibhaagasya
svasthasya mama sarvadaa..

## Translation
For me who am forever unmovable and indivisible, established in myself, there is no activity or inactivity, no liberation and no bondage.

---

# Verse 20.13

## Sanskrit
क्वोपदेशः क्व वा शास्त्रं
क्व शिष्यः क्व च वा गुरुः।
क्व चास्ति पुरुषार्थो वा
निरुपाधेः शिवस्य मे॥

## IAST
kvopadeshah kva vaa shaastram
kva shishyah kva cha vaa guruh.
kva chaasti purushaartho vaa
nirupaadheh shivasya me..

## Translation
For me who am blessed and without limitation, there is no initiation or scripture, no disciple or teacher, and no goal of human life.

---

# Verse 20.14

## Sanskrit
क्व चास्ति क्व च वा नास्ति
क्वास्ति चैकं क्व च द्वयं।
बहुनात्र किमुक्तेन
किंचिन्नोत्तिष्ठते मम॥

## IAST
kva chaasti kva cha vaa naasti
kvaasti chaikam kva cha dvayam.
bahunaatra kimuktena
kinchinnottisht'hate mama..

## Translation
There is no being or non-being, no unity or dualism. What more is there to say? There is nothing outside of me.
