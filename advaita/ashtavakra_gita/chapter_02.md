# Verse 2.1

## Sanskrit
जनक उवाच 
अहो निरंजनः शान्तो
बोधोऽहं प्रकृतेः परः।
एतावंतमहं कालं
मोहेनैव विडम्बितः॥

## IAST
janaka uvaacha 
aho niranjanah shaanto
bodho'ham prakriteh parah.
etaavantamaham kaalam
mohenaiva vid'ambitah..

## Translation
Janaka said: Truly I am spotless and at peace, the awareness beyond natural causality. All this time I have been afflicted by delusion.

---

# Verse 2.2

## Sanskrit
यथा प्रकाशयाम्येको
देहमेनो तथा जगत्।
अतो मम जगत्सर्वम-
थवा न च किंचन॥

## IAST
yathaa prakaashayaamyeko
dehameno tathaa jagat.
ato mama jagatsarvama-
thavaa na cha kinchana..

## Translation
As I alone give light to this body, so I do to the world. As a result the whole world is mine, or alternatively nothing is.

---

# Verse 2.3

## Sanskrit
सशरीरमहो विश्वं
परित्यज्य मयाऽधुना।
कुतश्चित् कौशलादेव
परमात्मा विलोक्यते॥

## IAST
sashareeramaho vishvam
parityajya mayaa'dhunaa.
kutashchit kaushalaadeva
paramaatmaa vilokyate..

## Translation
So now that I have abandoned the body and everything else, by good fortune my true self becomes apparent.

---

# Verse 2.4

## Sanskrit
यथा न तोयतो भिन्नास्-
तरंगाः फेन बुदबुदाः।
आत्मनो न तथा भिन्नं विश्वमात्मविनिर्गतम् ॥

## IAST
yathaa na toyato bhinnaas-
tarangaah phena budabudaah.
aatmano na tathaa bhinnam vishvamaatmavinirgatam ..

## Translation
Waves, foam, and bubbles do not differ from water. In the same way, all this which has emanated from oneself is no other than oneself.

---

# Verse 2.5

## Sanskrit
तंतुमात्रो भवेदेव
पटो यद्वद्विचारितः।
आत्मतन्मात्रमेवेदं
तद्वद्विश्वं विचारितम्॥

## IAST
tantumaatro bhavedeva
pat'o yadvadvichaaritah.
aatmatanmaatramevedam
tadvadvishvam vichaaritam..

## Translation
When you analyse it, cloth is found to be just thread. In the same way, when all this is analysed it is found to be no other than oneself.

---

# Verse 2.6

## Sanskrit
यथैवेक्षुरसे क्लृप्ता
तेन व्याप्तैव शर्करा।
तथा विश्वं मयि क्लृप्तं
मया व्याप्तं निरन्तरम्॥

## IAST
yathaivekshurase klriptaa
tena vyaaptaiva sharkaraa.
tathaa vishvam mayi klriptam
mayaa vyaaptam nirantaram..

## Translation
The sugar produced from the juice of the sugarcane is permeated throughout with the same taste. In the same way, all this, produced out of me, is completely permeated with myself.

---

# Verse 2.7

## Sanskrit
आत्माऽज्ञानाज्जगद्भाति
आत्मज्ञानान्न भासते।
रज्जवज्ञानादहिर्भाति
तज्ज्ञानाद्भासते न हि॥

## IAST
aatmaa'jnyaanaajjagadbhaati
aatmajnyaanaanna bhaasate.
rajjavajnyaanaadahirbhaati
tajjnyaanaadbhaasate na hi..

## Translation
From ignorance of oneself, the world appears, and by knowledge of oneself it appears no longer. From ignorance of the rope it appears to be a snake, and by knowledge of it it does so no longer.

---

# Verse 2.8

## Sanskrit
प्रकाशो मे निजं रूपं
नातिरिक्तोऽस्म्यहं ततः।
यदा प्रकाशते विश्वं
तदाऽहंभास एव हि॥

## IAST
prakaasho me nijam roopam
naatirikto'smyaham tatah.
yadaa prakaashate vishvam
tadaa'hambhaasa eva hi..

## Translation
Shining is my essential nature, and I am nothing other than that. When the world shines forth, it is only me that is shining forth.

---

# Verse 2.9

## Sanskrit
अहो विकल्पितं
विश्वंज्ञानान्मयि भासते।
रूप्यं शुक्तौ फणी रज्जौ
वारि सूर्यकरे यथा॥

## IAST
aho vikalpitam
vishvanjnyaanaanmayi bhaasate.
roopyam shuktau phanee rajjau
vaari sooryakare yathaa..

## Translation
All this appears in me imagined due to ignorance, just as a snake appears in the rope, the mirage of water in the sunlight, and silver in mother of pearl.

---

# Verse 2.10

## Sanskrit
मत्तो विनिर्गतं विश्वं
मय्येव लयमेष्यति।
मृदि कुम्भो जले वीचिः
कनके कटकं यथा॥

## IAST
matto vinirgatam vishvam
mayyeva layameshyati.
mridi kumbho jale veechih
kanake kat'akam yathaa..

## Translation
All this, which has originated out of me, is resolved back into me too, like a jug back into clay, a wave into water, and a bracelet into gold.

---

# Verse 2.11

## Sanskrit
अहो अहं नमो मह्यं
विनाशो यस्य नास्ति मे।
ब्रह्मादिस्तंबपर्यन्तं
जगन्नाशोऽपि तिष्ठतः॥

## IAST
aho aham namo mahyam
vinaasho yasya naasti me.
brahmaadistambaparyantam
yagannaasho'pi tisht'hatah..

## Translation
How wonderful I am! Glory to me, for whom there is no destruction, remaining even beyond the destruction of the world from Brahma down to the last clump of grass.

---

# Verse 2.12

## Sanskrit
अहो अहं नमो मह्यं
एकोऽहं देहवानपि।
क्वचिन्न गन्ता नागन्ता
व्याप्य विश्वमवस्थितः॥

## IAST
aho aham namo mahyam
eko'ham dehavaanapi.
kvachinna gantaa naagantaa
vyaapya vishvamavasthitah..

## Translation
How wonderful I am! Glory to me, solitary even though with a body, neither going or coming anywhere, I who abide forever, filling all that is.

---

# Verse 2.13

## Sanskrit
अहो अहं नमो मह्यं
दक्षो नास्तीह मत्समः।
असंस्पृश्य शरीरेण
येन विश्वं चिरं धृतम्॥

## IAST
aho aham namo mahyam
daksho naasteeha matsamah.
asamsprishya shareerena
yena vishvam chiram dhritam..

## Translation
How wonderful I am! Glory to me! There is no one so clever as me! I who have borne all that is forever, without even touching it with my body!

---

# Verse 2.14

## Sanskrit
अहो अहं नमो मह्यं
यस्य मे नास्ति किंचन।
अथवा यस्य मे सर्वं यद्
वाङ्मनसगोचरम्॥

## IAST
aho aham namo mahyam
yasya me naasti kinchana.
athavaa yasya me sarvam yad
vaangmanasagocharam..

## Translation
How wonderful I am! Glory to me! I who possess nothing at all, or alternatively possess everything that speech and mind can refer to.

---

# Verse 2.15

## Sanskrit
ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं तथा ज्ञाता
त्रितयं नास्ति वास्तवं।
अज्ञानाद् भाति यत्रेदं
सोऽहमस्मि निरंजनः॥

## IAST
jnyaanam jnyeyam tathaa jnyaataa
tritayam naasti vaastavam.
ajnyaanaad bhaati yatredam
so'hamasmi niranjanah..

## Translation
Knowledge, what is to be known, and the knower — these three do not exist in reality. I am the spotless reality in which they appear because of ignorance.

---

# Verse 2.16

## Sanskrit
द्वैतमूलमहो दुःखं नान्य-
त्तस्याऽस्ति भेषजं।
दृश्यमेतन् मृषा सर्वं
एकोऽहं चिद्रसोमलः॥

## IAST
dvaitamoolamaho duhkham naanya-
ttasyaa'sti bheshajam.
drishyametan mrishaa sarvam
eko'ham chidrasomalah..

## Translation
Truly dualism is the root of suffering. There is no other remedy for it than the realisation that all this that we see is unreal, and that I am the one stainless reality, consisting of consciousness.

---

# Verse 2.17

## Sanskrit
बोधमात्रोऽहमज्ञानाद्
उपाधिः कल्पितो मया।
एवं विमृशतो नित्यं
निर्विकल्पे स्थितिर्मम॥

## IAST
bodhamaatro'hamajnyaanaad
upaadhih kalpito mayaa.
evam vimrishato nityam
nirvikalpe sthitirmama..

## Translation
I am pure awareness though through ignorance I have imagined myself to have additional attributes. By continually reflecting like this, my dwelling place is in the Unimagined.

---

# Verse 2.18

## Sanskrit
न मे बन्धोऽस्ति मोक्षो वा
भ्रान्तिः शान्तो निराश्रया।
अहो मयि स्थितं विश्वं
वस्तुतो न मयि स्थितम्॥

## IAST
na me bandho'sti moksho vaa
bhraantih shaanto niraashrayaa.
aho mayi sthitam vishvam
vastuto na mayi sthitam..

## Translation
For me here is neither bondage nor liberation. The illusion has lost its basis and ceased. Truly all this exists in me, though ultimately it does not even exist in me.

---

# Verse 2.19

## Sanskrit
सशरीरमिदं विश्वं
न किंचिदिति निश्चितं।
शुद्धचिन्मात्र आत्मा च
तत्कस्मिन् कल्पनाधुना॥

## IAST
sashareeramidam vishvam
na kinchiditi nishchitam.
shuddhachinmaatra aatmaa cha
tatkasmin kalpanaadhunaa..

## Translation
Recognising that all this and my body too are nothing, while my true self is nothing but pure consciousness, what is there left for the imagination to work on now?

---

# Verse 2.20

## Sanskrit
शरीरं स्वर्गनरकौ
बन्धमोक्षौ भयं तथा।
कल्पनामात्रमेवैतत्
किं मे कार्यं चिदात्मनः॥

## IAST
shareeram svarganarakau
bandhamokshau bhayam tathaa.
kalpanaamaatramevaitat
kim me kaaryam chidaatmanah..

## Translation
The body, heaven and hell, bondage and liberation, and fear too, all this is pure imagination. What is there left to do for me whose very nature is consciousness?

---

# Verse 2.21

## Sanskrit
अहो जनसमूहेऽपि
न द्वैतं पश्यतो मम।
अरण्यमिव संवृत्तं
क्व रतिं करवाण्यहम्॥

## IAST
aho janasamoohe'pi
na dvaitam pashyato mama.
aranyamiva samvrittam
kva ratim karavaanyaham..

## Translation
I do not even see dualism in a crowd of people, so what do I gain if it is replaced by a desert?

---

# Verse 2.22

## Sanskrit
नाहं देहो न मे देहो
जीवो नाहमहं हि चित्।
अयमेव हि मे बन्ध
आसीद्या जीविते स्पृहा॥

## IAST
naaham deho na me deho
yeevo naahamaham hi chit.
ayameva hi me bandha
aaseedyaa jeevite sprihaa..

## Translation
I am not the body, nor is the body mine. I am not a living being. I am consciousness. It was my thirst for living that was my bondage.

---

# Verse 2.23

## Sanskrit
अहो भुवनकल्लोलै-
र्विचित्रैर्द्राक् समुत्थितं।
मय्यनंतमहांभोधौ
चित्तवाते समुद्यते॥

## IAST
aho bhuvanakallolai-
rvichitrairdraak samutthitam.
mayyanantamahaambhodhau
chittavaate samudyate..

## Translation
Truly it is in the infinite ocean of myself, that, stimulated by the colourful waves of the world, everything suddenly arises in the wind of consciousness.

---

# Verse 2.24

## Sanskrit
मय्यनंतमहांभोधौ
चित्तवाते प्रशाम्यति।
अभाग्याज्जीववणिजो
जगत्पोतो विनश्वरः॥

## IAST
mayyanantamahaambhodhau
chittavaate prashaamyati.
abhaagyaajjeevavanijo
yagatpoto vinashvarah..

## Translation
In the infinite ocean of myself, the wind of thought subsides, and the world boat of the living-being traders is wrecked by lack of goods.

---

# Verse 2.25

## Sanskrit
मय्यनन्तमहांभोधा-
वाश्चर्यं जीववीचयः।
उद्यन्ति घ्नन्ति खेलन्ति
प्रविशन्ति स्वभावतः॥

## IAST
mayyanantamahaambhodhaa-
vaashcharyam jeevaveechayah.
udyanti ghnanti khelanti
pravishanti svabhaavatah..

## Translation
How wonderful it is that in the infinite ocean of myself the waves of living beings arise, collide, play, and disappear, in accordance with their nature.
