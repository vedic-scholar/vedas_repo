# Verse 9.1

## Sanskrit
अष्टावक्र उवाच 
कृताकृते च द्वन्द्वानि
कदा शान्तानि कस्य वा।
एवं ज्ञात्वेह निर्वेदाद् भव
त्यागपरोऽव्रती॥

## IAST
asht'aavakra uvaacha 
kritaakrite cha dvandvaani
kadaa shaantaani kasya vaa.
evam jnyaatveha nirvedaad bhava
tyaagaparo'vratee..

## Translation
Ashtavakra said: Knowing when the dualism of things done and undone has been put to rest, or the person for whom they occur has, then you can here and now go beyond renunciation and obligations by indifference to such things.

# Verse 9.2

## Sanskrit
कस्यापि तात धन्यस्य लोकचेष्टावलोकनात्।
जीवितेच्छा बुभुक्षा च
बुभुत्सोपशमं गताः॥

## IAST
kasyaapi taata dhanyasya lokachesht'aavalokanaat.
yeevitechchhaa bubhukshaa cha
bubhutsopashamam gataah..

## Translation
Rare indeed, my son, is the lucky man whose observation of the world’s behaviour has led to the extinction of his thirst for living, thirst for pleasure, and thirst for knowledge.

---

# Verse 9.3

## Sanskrit
अनित्यं सर्वमेवेदं
तापत्रयदूषितं।
असारं निन्दितं हेयमि-
ति निश्चित्य शाम्यति॥

## IAST
anityam sarvamevedam
taapatrayadooshitam.
asaaram ninditam heyami-
ti nishchitya shaamyati..

## Translation
All this is transient and spoiled by the three sorts of pain. Knowing it to be insubstantial, ignoble, and fit only for rejection, one attains peace.

---

# Verse 9.4

## Sanskrit
कोऽसौ कालो वयः किं वा
यत्र द्वन्द्वानि नो नृणां।
तान्युपेक्ष्य यथाप्राप्तवर्ती
सिद्धिमवाप्नुयात्॥

## IAST
ko'sau kaalo vayah kim vaa
yatra dvandvaani no nrinaam.
taanyupekshya yathaapraaptavartee
siddhimavaapnuyaat..

## Translation
When was that age or time of life when the dualism of extremes did not exist for men? Abandoning them, a person who is happy to take whatever comes attains perfection.

---

# Verse 9.5

## Sanskrit
नाना मतं महर्षीणां
साधूनां योगिनां तथा।
दृष्ट्वा निर्वेदमापन्नः
को न शाम्यति मानवः॥

## IAST
naanaa matam maharsheenaam
saadhoonaam yoginaam tathaa.
drisht'vaa nirvedamaapannah
ko na shaamyati maanavah..

## Translation
Who does not end up with indifference to such things and attain peace when he has seen the differences of opinions among the great sages, saints, and yogis?

---

# Verse 9.6

## Sanskrit
कृत्वा मूर्तिपरिज्ञानं
चैतन्यस्य न किं गुरुः।
निर्वेदसमतायुक्त्या
यस्तारयति संसृतेः॥

## IAST
kritvaa moortiparijnyaanam
chaitanyasya na kim guruh.
nirvedasamataayuktyaa
yastaarayati samsriteh..

## Translation
Is he not a guru who, endowed with dispassion and equanimity, achieves full knowledge of the nature of consciousness, and leads others out of samsara?

---

# Verse 9.7

## Sanskrit
पश्य भूतविकारांस्त्वं
भूतमात्रान् यथार्थतः।
तत्क्षणाद् बन्धनिर्मुक्तः
स्वरूपस्थो भविष्यसि॥

## IAST
pashya bhootavikaaraamstvam
bhootamaatraan yathaarthatah.
tatkshanaad bandhanirmuktah
svaroopastho bhavishyasi..

## Translation
If you would just see the transformations of the elements as nothing more than the elements, then you would immediately be freed from all bonds and established in your own nature.

---

# Verse 9.8

## Sanskrit
वासना एव संसार इति
सर्वा विमुंच ताः।
तत्त्यागो वासनात्यागा-
त्स्थितिरद्य यथा तथा॥

## IAST
vaasanaa eva samsaara iti
sarvaa vimuncha taah.
tattyaago vaasanaatyaagaa-
tsthitiradya yathaa tathaa..

## Translation
One’s desires are samsara. Knowing this, abandon them. The renunciation of them is the renunciation of it. Now you can remain as you are.
