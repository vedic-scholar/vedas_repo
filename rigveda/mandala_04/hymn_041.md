# Verse 1

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
WHAT laud, O Indra-Varuṇa, with oblation, hath like the Immortal Priest obtained your favour? Hath our effectual laud, addressed with homage, touched you, O Indra-Varuṇa, in spirit?

# Verse 2

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
He who with dainty food hath won you, Indra and Varuṇa, Gods, as his allies to friendship, Slayeth the Vṛtras and his foes in battles, and through your mighty favours is made famous.

---

# Verse 3

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
Indra and Varuṇa are most liberal givers of treasure to the men who toil to serve them, When they, as Friends inclined to friendship, honoured with dainty food, delight in flowing Soma.

---

# Verse 4

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
Indra and Varuṇa, ye hurl, O Mighty, on him your strongest flashing bolt of thunder Who treats us ill, the robber and oppressor: measure on him your overwhelming vigour.

---

# Verse 5

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
O Indra-Varuṇa, be ye the lovers of this my song, as steers who love the milch-Cow. Milk may it yield us as, gone forth to pasture, the great Cow pouring out her thousand rivers.

---

# Verse 6

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
For fertile fields, for worthy sons and grandsons, for the Sun's beauty and for steer-like vigour, May Indra-Varuṇa with gracious favours work marvels for us in the stress of battle.

---

# Verse 7

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
For you, as Princes, for your ancient kindness, good comrades of the man who seeks for booty, We choose to us for the dear bond of friendship, most liberal Heroes bringing bliss like parents.

---

# Verse 8

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
Showing their strength, these hymns for grace, Free-givers I have gone to you, devoted, as to battle. For glory have they gone, as milk to Soma, to Indra-Varuṇa my thoughts and praises.

---

# Verse 9

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
To Indra and to Varuṇa, desirous of gaining wealth have these my thoughts proceeded. They have come nigh to you as treasure-lovers, like mares, fleet-footed, eager for the glory.

---

# Verse 10

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
May we ourselves be lords of during riches, of ample sustenance for car and hones. So may the Twain who work with newest succours bring yoked teams hitherward to us and riches.

---

# Verse 11

## Sanskrit
[Add Devanagari Sanskrit]

## IAST
[Add IAST Transliteration]

## Translation
Come with your mighty succours, O ye Mighty; come, Indra-Varuṇa, to us in battle. What time the flashing arrows play in combat, may we through you be winners in the contest.
