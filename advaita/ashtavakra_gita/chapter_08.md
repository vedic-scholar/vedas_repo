# Verse 8.1

## Sanskrit
अष्टावक्र उवाच 
तदा बन्धो यदा चित्तं
किन्चिद् वांछति शोचति।
किंचिन् मुंचति गृण्हाति
किंचिद् हृष्यति कुप्यति॥

## IAST
asht'aavakra uvaacha 
tadaa bandho yadaa chittam
kinchid vaanchhati shochati.
kinchin munchati grinhaati
kinchid hrishyati kupyati..

## Translation
Ashtavakra said: Bondage is when the mind longs for something, grieves about something, rejects something, holds on to something, is pleased about something or displeased about something.

---

# Verse 8.2

## Sanskrit
तदा मुक्तिर्यदा चित्तं
न वांछति न शोचति।
न मुंचति न गृण्हाति
न हृष्यति न कुप्यति॥

## IAST
tadaa muktiryadaa chittam
na vaanchhati na shochati.
na munchati na grinhaati
na hrishyati na kupyati..

## Translation
Liberation is when the mind does not long for anything, grieve about anything, reject anything, or hold on to anything, and is not pleased about anything or displeased about anything.

---

# Verse 8.3

## Sanskrit
तदा बन्धो यदा चित्तं
सक्तं काश्वपि दृष्टिषु।
तदा मोक्षो यदा चित्तम-
सक्तं सर्वदृष्टिषु॥

## IAST
tadaa bandho yadaa chittam
saktam kaashvapi drisht'ishu.
tadaa moksho yadaa chittama-
saktam sarvadrisht'ishu..

## Translation
Bondage is when the mind is tangled in one of the senses, and liberation is when the mind is not tangled in any of the senses.

---

# Verse 8.4

## Sanskrit
यदा नाहं तदा मोक्षो
यदाहं बन्धनं तदा।
मत्वेति हेलया किंचिन्-
मा गृहाण विमुंच मा॥

## IAST
yadaa naaham tadaa moksho
yadaaham bandhanam tadaa.
matveti helayaa kinchin-
maa grihaana vimuncha maa..

## Translation
When there is no “me,” that is liberation, and when there is “me” there is bondage. Consider this carefully, and neither hold on to anything nor reject anything.
