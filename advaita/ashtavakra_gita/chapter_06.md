# Verse 6.1

## Sanskrit
अष्टावक्र उवाच 
आकाशवदनन्तोऽहं
घटवत् प्राकृतं जगत्।
इति ज्ञानं तथैतस्य
न त्यागो न ग्रहो लयः॥

## IAST
asht'aavakra uvaacha 
aakaashavadananto'ham
ghat'avat praakritam jagat.
iti jnyaanam tathaitasya
na tyaago na graho layah..

## Translation
Ashtavakra said: I am infinite like space, and the natural world is like a jar. To know this is knowledge, and then there is neither renunciation, acceptance, or cessation of it.

# Verse 6.2

## Sanskrit
महोदधिरिवाहं स
प्रपंचो वीचिसऽन्निभः।
इति ज्ञानं तथैतस्य
न त्यागो न ग्रहो लयः॥

## IAST
mahodadhirivaaham sa
prapancho veechisa'nnibhah.
iti jnyaanam tathaitasya
na tyaago na graho layah..

## Translation
I am like the ocean, and the multiplicity of objects is comparable to a wave. To know this is knowledge, and then there is neither renunciation, acceptance or cessation of it.

---

# Verse 6.3

## Sanskrit
अहं स शुक्तिसङ्काशो
रूप्यवद् विश्वकल्पना।
इति ज्ञानं तथैतस्य
न त्यागो न ग्रहो लयः॥

## IAST
aham sa shuktisankaasho
roopyavad vishvakalpanaa.
iti jnyaanam tathaitasya
na tyaago na graho layah..

## Translation
I am like the mother of pearl, and the imagined world is like the silver. To know this is knowledge, and then there is neither renunciation, acceptance, or cessation of it.

---

# Verse 6.4

## Sanskrit
अहं वा सर्वभूतेषु
सर्वभूतान्यथो मयि।
इति ज्ञानं तथैतस्य
न त्यागो न ग्रहो लयः॥

## IAST
aham vaa sarvabhooteshu
sarvabhootaanyatho mayi.
iti jnyaanam tathaitasya
na tyaago na graho layah..

## Translation
Alternatively, I am in all beings, and all beings are in me. To know this is knowledge, and then there is neither renunciation, acceptance, or cessation of it.
