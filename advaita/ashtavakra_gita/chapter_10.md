category: "Advaita Vedanta"
chapter: "Chapter 10: Vairagya"
subtitle: "Dispassion"
synopsis: "Ashtavakra enjoins Janaka to eschew into 'Aram' (virtuous conduct), 'Porul' (wealth), 'Inbam' (enjoyment) – all transitory and unreal – and thus freed of desire and therefore action stilled to rest only in bliss."
---

# Verse 10.1

## Sanskrit
अष्टावक्र उवाच 
विहाय वैरिणं कामम-
र्थं चानर्थसंकुलं।
धर्ममप्येतयोर्हेतुं
सर्वत्रानादरं कुरु॥

## IAST
asht'aavakra uvaacha 
vihaaya vairinam kaamama-
rtham chaanarthasankulam.
dharmamapyetayorhetum
sarvatraanaadaram kuru..

## Translation
Ashtavakra said: Abandon desire, the enemy, along with gain, itself so full of loss, and the good deeds which are the cause of the other two — practice indifference to everything.

---

# Verse 10.2

## Sanskrit
स्वप्नेन्द्रजालवत् पश्य
दिनानि त्रीणि पंच वा।
मित्रक्षेत्रधनागार-
दारदायादिसंपदः॥

## IAST
svapnendrajaalavat pashya
dinaani treeni pancha vaa.
mitrakshetradhanaagaara-
daaradaayaadisampadah..

## Translation
Look on such things as land, friends, money, property, wife, and bequests as nothing but a dream or a magician’s show lasting three or five days.

---

# Verse 10.3

## Sanskrit
यत्र यत्र भवेत्तृष्णा
संसारं विद्धि तत्र वै।
प्रौढवैराग्यमाश्रित्य
वीततृष्णः सुखी भव॥

## IAST
yatra yatra bhavettrishnaa
samsaaram viddhi tatra vai.
praud'havairaagyamaashritya
veetatrishnah sukhee bhava..

## Translation
Wherever a desire occurs, see samsara in it. Establishing yourself in firm dispassion, be free of passion and happy.

---

# Verse 10.4

## Sanskrit
तृष्णामात्रात्मको बन्धस्-
तन्नाशो मोक्ष उच्यते।
भवासंसक्तिमात्रेण प्राप्तितुष्टिर्मुहुर्मुहुः॥

## IAST
trishnaamaatraatmako bandhas-
tannaasho moksha uchyate.
bhavaasamsaktimaatrena praaptitusht'irmuhurmuhuh..

## Translation
The essential nature of bondage is nothing other than desire, and its elimination is known as liberation. It is simply by not being attached to changing things that the everlasting joy of attainment is reached.

---

# Verse 10.5

## Sanskrit
त्वमेकश्चेतनः शुद्धो
जडं विश्वमसत्तथा।
अविद्यापि न किंचित्सा
का बुभुत्सा तथापि ते॥

## IAST
tvamekashchetanah shuddho
yad'am vishvamasattathaa.
avidyaapi na kinchitsaa
kaa bubhutsaa tathaapi te..

## Translation
You are one, conscious and pure, while all this is inert non-being. Ignorance itself is nothing, so what is the point of wanting to understand?

---

# Verse 10.6

## Sanskrit
राज्यं सुताः कलत्राणि
शरीराणि सुखानि च।
संसक्तस्यापि नष्टानि
तव जन्मनि जन्मनि॥

## IAST
raajyam sutaah kalatraani
shareeraani sukhaani cha.
samsaktasyaapi nasht'aani
tava janmani janmani..

## Translation
Kingdoms, children, wives, bodies, pleasures — these have all been lost to you life after life, attached to them though you were.

---

# Verse 10.7

## Sanskrit
अलमर्थेन कामेन
सुकृतेनापि कर्मणा।
एभ्यः संसारकान्तारे
न विश्रान्तमभून् मनः॥

## IAST
alamarthena kaamena
sukritenaapi karmanaa.
ebhyah samsaarakaantaare
na vishraantamabhoon manah..

## Translation
Enough of wealth, sensuality, and good deeds. In the forest of samsara the mind has never found satisfaction in these.

---

# Verse 10.8

## Sanskrit
कृतं न कति जन्मानि
कायेन मनसा गिरा।
दुःखमायासदं कर्म
तदद्याप्युपरम्यताम्॥

## IAST
kritam na kati janmaani
kaayena manasaa giraa.
duhkhamaayaasadam karma
tadadyaapyuparamyataam..

## Translation
How many births have you not done hard and painful labour with body, mind, and speech. Now at last, stop!
