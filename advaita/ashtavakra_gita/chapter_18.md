---
title: "Ashtavakra Gita"
category: "Advaita Vedanta"
chapter: "Chapter 18: Jivanmukti"
subtitle: "Way and Goal of Natural Samadhi"
synopsis: "Abidance in one's natural state of quiescence by renouncing everything else is bliss. All other sadhanas are first for novices. One who abides as self stands apart from the ways of the world duty, dualities and mistaken thoughts are not for hum. His nature is wonderful. Freedom from bondage alone bestows this exalted state. A jnani the 'dhira' stands heads and shoulders above the hapless ignorant. The bond-free natural state of a jnani is more beatific than the rulebound state of a sadhak. Only a jnani can know another jnani. He abides forever in the non-dual state with no 'other' or a second- the cause of attachment and aversion. Viewing equally life and death, solitude and maddening crowd he will be anchored firmly in the true content. (Thus Ashtavakra expatriates)"
---

# Verse 18.1

## Sanskrit
अष्टावक्र उवाच 
यस्य बोधोदये तावत्-
स्वप्नवद् भवति भ्रमः।
तस्मै सुखैकरूपाय
नमः शान्ताय तेजसे॥

## IAST
asht'aavakra uvaacha 
yasya bodhodaye taavat-
svapnavad bhavati bhramah.
tasmai sukhaikaroopaaya
namah shaantaaya tejase..

## Translation
Ashtavakra said: Praise be to That by the awareness of which delusion itself becomes dream-like, to that which is pure happiness, peace, and light.

# Verse 18.2

## Sanskrit
अर्जयित्वाखिलान् अर्थान्
भोगानाप्नोति पुष्कलान्।
न हि सर्वपरित्याजम-
न्तरेण सुखी भवेत्॥

## IAST

bhogaanaapnoti pushkalaan.
na hi sarvaparityaajama-
ntarena sukhee bhavet..

## Translation
One may get all sorts of pleasure by the acquisition of various objects of enjoyment, but one cannot be happy except by the renunciation of everything.

# Verse 18.3

## Sanskrit

## IAST

## Translation
How can there be happiness, for one who has been burnt inside by the blistering sun of the pain of thinking that there are things that still need doing, without the rain of the nectar of peace?

## Sanskrit

## Translation
The realm of one’s self is not far away, nor can it be achieved by the addition of limitations to its nature. It is unimaginable, effortless, unchanging, and spotless.

## Translation

## Translation
Knowing himself to be God, and being and non-being just imagination, what should the man free from desire learn, say, or do?

# Verse 18.9

## Sanskrit

## Translation

## Translation
For the yogi who has found peace, there is no distraction or one-pointedness, no higher knowledge or ignorance, no pleasure and no pain.

The dominion of heaven or beggary, gain or loss, life among men or in the forest, these make no difference to a yogi whose nature it is to be free from distinctions.

There are no religious obligations, wealth, sensuality, or discrimination for a yogi free from such opposites as “I have done this,” and “I have not done that.”

क्व तमः क्व प्रकाशो वा
हानं क्व च न किंचन।
निर्विकारस्य धीरस्य
निरातंकस्य सर्वदा॥

## IAST
kva tamah kva prakaasho vaa
haanam kva cha na kinchana.
nirvikaarasya dheerasya
niraatankasya sarvadaa..

## Translation
For the wise man who is always unchanging and fearless there is neither darkness nor light nor destruction nor anything.

---

# Verse 18.79

## Sanskrit
क्व धैर्यं क्व विवेकित्वं
क्व निरातंकतापि वा।
अनिर्वाच्यस्वभावस्य
निःस्वभावस्य योगिनः॥

## IAST
kva dhairyam kva vivekitvam
kva niraatankataapi vaa.
anirvaachyasvabhaavasya
nihsvabhaavasya yoginah..

## Translation
There is neither fortitude, prudence, nor courage for the yogi whose nature is beyond description and free of individuality.

---

# Verse 18.80

## Sanskrit
न स्वर्गो नैव नरको
जीवन्मुक्तिर्न चैव हि।
बहुनात्र किमुक्तेन
योगदृष्ट्या न किंचन॥

## IAST
na svargo naiva narako
yeevanmuktirna chaiva hi.
bahunaatra kimuktena
yogadrisht'yaa na kinchana..

## Translation
There is neither heaven nor hell nor even liberation during life. In a nutshell, in the sight of the seer nothing exists at all.

---

# Verse 18.81

## Sanskrit
नैव प्रार्थयते लाभं
नालाभेनानुशोचति।
धीरस्य शीतलं चित्तम-
मृतेनैव पूरितम्॥

## IAST
naiva praarthayate laabham
naalaabhenaanushochati.
dheerasya sheetalam chittama-
mritenaiva pooritam..

## Translation
He neither longs for possessions nor grieves at their absence. The calm mind of the sage is full of the nectar of immortality.

---

# Verse 18.82

## Sanskrit
न शान्तं स्तौति निष्कामो
न दुष्टमपि निन्दति।
समदुःखसुखस्तृप्तः किंचित्
कृत्यं न पश्यति॥

## IAST
na shaantam stauti nishkaamo
na dusht'amapi nindati.
samaduhkhasukhastriptah kinchit
krityam na pashyati..

## Translation
The dispassionate man does not praise the good or blame the wicked. Content and equal in pain and pleasure, he sees nothing that needs doing.

---

# Verse 18.83

## Sanskrit
धीरो न द्वेष्टि संसारमा-
त्मानं न दिदृक्षति।
हर्षामर्षविनिर्मुक्तो न
मृतो न च जीवति॥

## IAST
dheero na dvesht'i samsaaramaa-
tmaanam na didrikshati.
harshaamarshavinirmukto na
mrito na cha jeevati..

## Translation
The wise man is not averse to samsara, nor does he seek to know himself. Free from pleasure and impatience, he is not dead and he is not alive.

---

# Verse 18.84

## Sanskrit
निःस्नेहः पुत्रदारादौ
निष्कामो विषयेषु च।
निश्चिन्तः स्वशरीरेऽपि
निराशः शोभते बुधः॥

## IAST
nihsnehah putradaaraadau
nishkaamo vishayeshu cha.
nishchintah svashareere'pi
niraashah shobhate budhah..

## Translation
The wise man excels by being free from anticipation, without attachment to such things as children or wives, free from desire for the senses, and not even concerned about his own body.

---

# Verse 18.85

## Sanskrit
तुष्टिः सर्वत्र धीरस्य
यथापतितवर्तिनः।
स्वच्छन्दं चरतो देशान्
यत्रस्तमितशायिनः॥

## IAST
tusht'ih sarvatra dheerasya
yathaapatitavartinah.
svachchhandam charato deshaan
yatrastamitashaayinah..

## Translation
The wise man, who lives on whatever happens to come to him, roams wherever he pleases, and sleeps wherever the sun happens to set, is at peace everywhere.

---

# Verse 18.86

## Sanskrit
पततूदेतु वा देहो नास्य
चिन्ता महात्मनः।
स्वभावभूमिविश्रान्ति-
विस्मृताशेषसंसृतेः॥

## IAST
patatoodetu vaa deho naasya
chintaa mahaatmanah.
svabhaavabhoomivishraanti-
vismritaasheshasamsriteh..

## Translation
Whether his body rises or falls, the great-souled one gives it no thought, having forgotten all about samsara in coming to rest on the ground of his true nature.

---

# Verse 18.87

## Sanskrit
अकिंचनः कामचारो
निर्द्वन्द्वश्छिन्नसंशयः।
असक्तः सर्वभावेषु
केवलो रमते बुधः॥

## IAST
akinchanah kaamachaaro
nirdvandvashchhinnasamshayah.
asaktah sarvabhaaveshu
kevalo ramate budhah..

## Translation
The wise man has the joy of being complete in himself and without possessions, acting as he pleases, free from duality and rid of doubts, and without attachment to any creature.

---

# Verse 18.88

## Sanskrit
निर्ममः शोभते धीरः
समलोष्टाश्मकांचनः।
सुभिन्नहृदयग्रन्थि-
र्विनिर्धूतरजस्तमः॥

## IAST
nirmamah shobhate dheerah
samalosht'aashmakaanchanah.
subhinnahridayagranthi-
rvinirdhootarajastamah..

## Translation
The wise man excels in being without the sense of “me”. Earth, a stone, or gold are the same to him. The knots of his heart have been rent asunder, and he is freed from greed and blindness.

---

# Verse 18.89

## Sanskrit
सर्वत्रानवधानस्य न
किंचिद् वासना हृदि।
मुक्तात्मनो वितृप्तस्य
तुलना केन जायते॥

## IAST
sarvatraanavadhaanasya na
kinchid vaasanaa hridi.
muktaatmano vitriptasya
tulanaa kena jaayate..

## Translation
Who can compare with that contented, liberated soul who pays no regard to anything and has no desire left in his heart?

---

# Verse 18.90

## Sanskrit
जानन्नपि न जानाति
पश्यन्नपि न पश्यति।
ब्रुवन्न् अपि न च ब्रूते
कोऽन्यो निर्वासनादृते॥

## IAST
jaanannapi na jaanaati
pashyannapi na pashyati.
bruvann api na cha broote
ko'nyo nirvaasanaadrite..

## Translation
Who but the upright man without desire knows without knowing, sees without seeing, and speaks without speaking?

---

# Verse 18.91

## Sanskrit
भिक्षुर्वा भूपतिर्वापि यो
निष्कामः स शोभते।
भावेषु गलिता यस्य
शोभनाशोभना मतिः॥

## IAST
bhikshurvaa bhoopatirvaapi yo
nishkaamah sa shobhate.
bhaaveshu galitaa yasya
shobhanaashobhanaa matih..

## Translation
Beggar or king, he excels who is without desire, and whose opinion of things is rid of “good” and “bad.”

# Verse 18.92

## Sanskrit
क्व स्वाच्छन्द्यं क्व संकोचः
क्व वा तत्त्वविनिश्चयः।
निर्व्याजार्जवभूतस्य
चरितार्थस्य योगिनः॥

## IAST
kva svaachchhandyam kva sankochah
kva vaa tattvavinishchayah.
nirvyaajaarjavabhootasya
charitaarthasya yoginah..

## Translation
There is neither dissolute behaviour nor virtue, nor even discrimination of the truth for the sage who has reached the goal and is the very embodiment of guileless sincerity.

# Verse 18.93

## Sanskrit
आत्मविश्रान्तितृप्तेन
निराशेन गतार्तिना।
अन्तर्यदनुभूयेत तत्
कथं कस्य कथ्यते॥

## IAST
aatmavishraantitriptena
niraashena gataartinaa.
antaryadanubhooyeta tat
katham kasya kathyate..

## Translation
That which is experienced within by one who is desireless and free from pain, and content to rest in himself — how could it be described, and of whom?

# Verse 18.94

## Sanskrit
सुप्तोऽपि न सुषुप्तौ च
स्वप्नेऽपि शयितो न च।
जागरेऽपि न जागर्ति
धीरस्तृप्तः पदे पदे॥

## IAST
supto'pi na sushuptau cha
svapne'pi shayito na cha.
yaagare'pi na jaagarti
dheerastriptah pade pade..

## Translation
The wise man who is contented in all circumstances is not asleep even in deep sleep, nor sleeping in a dream, nor waking when he is awake.

# Verse 18.95

## Sanskrit
ज्ञः सचिन्तोऽपि निश्चिन्तः
सेन्द्रियोऽपि निरिन्द्रियः।
सुबुद्धिरपि निर्बुद्धिः
साहंकारोऽनहङ्कृतिः॥

## IAST
jnyah sachinto'pi nishchintah
sendriyo'pi nirindriyah.
subuddhirapi nirbuddhih
saahankaaro'nahankritih..

## Translation
The seer is without thoughts even when thinking, without senses among the senses, without understanding even in understanding, and without a sense of responsibility even in the ego.

# Verse 18.96

## Sanskrit

न विरक्तो न संगवान्।
न मुमुक्षुर्न वा मुक्ता

## IAST
na sukhee na cha vaa duhkhee
na virakto na sangavaan.
na mumukshurna vaa muktaa
na kinchinnna cha kinchana..

## Translation
Neither happy nor unhappy, neither detached nor attached, neither seeking liberation nor liberated, he is neither something nor nothing.

# Verse 18.97

## Sanskrit
विक्षेपेऽपि न विक्षिप्तः
समाधौ न समाधिमान्।
जाड्येऽपि न जडो धन्यः
पाण्डित्येऽपि न पण्डितः॥

## IAST
vikshepe'pi na vikshiptah
samaadhau na samaadhimaan.
yaad'ye'pi na jad'o dhanyah
paand'itye'pi na pand'itah..

## Translation
Not distracted in distraction, in mental stillness not poised, in stupidity not stupid, that blessed one is not even wise in his wisdom.

# Verse 18.98

## Sanskrit
मुक्तो यथास्थितिस्वस्थः
कृतकर्तव्यनिर्वृतः।
समः सर्वत्र वैतृष्ण्यान्न
स्मरत्यकृतं कृतम्॥

## IAST
mukto yathaasthitisvasthah
kritakartavyanirvritah.
samah sarvatra vaitrishnyaanna
smaratyakritam kritam..

## Translation
The liberated man is self-possessed in all circumstances and free from the idea of “done” and “still to do.” He is the same wherever he is and without greed. He does not dwell on what he has done or not done.

# Verse 18.99

## Sanskrit
न प्रीयते वन्द्यमानो
निन्द्यमानो न कुप्यति।
नैवोद्विजति मरणे
जीवने नाभिनन्दति॥

## IAST
na preeyate vandyamaano
nindyamaano na kupyati.
naivodvijati marane
yeevane naabhinandati..

## Translation
He is not pleased when praised nor upset when blamed. He is not afraid of death nor attached to life.

# Verse 18.100

## Sanskrit
न धावति जनाकीर्णं
नारण्यं उपशान्तधीः।
यथातथा यत्रतत्र
सम एवावतिष्ठते॥

## IAST
na dhaavati janaakeernam
naaranyam upashaantadheeh.
yathaatathaa yatratatra
sama evaavatisht'hate..

## Translation
A man at peace does not run off to popular resorts or to the forest. Whatever and wherever, he remains the same.
