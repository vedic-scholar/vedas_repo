# Verse 1

## Sanskrit
वाय॑वा या॑हि दर्शते॒मे सोमा॑ अरं॒कृता॑: ।
तेषां॑ पाहि श्रु॒धी हव॑म् ॥ १ ॥

## IAST
vāyavā yāhi darṣateme somā araṅkṛtāḥ |
teṣāṃ pāhi śrudhī havam || 1 ||

## Translation
Beautiful Vayu, come! These Soma juices are prepared for thee; drink of them, and hear our call.

---

# Verse 2

## Sanskrit
वाय॑ उ॒क्थेभि॑र्जरन्ते॒ त्वामच्छा॑ जरितार॑: ।
सु॒तसो॑मा अह॒र्विदा॑ ॥ २ ॥

## IAST
vāya ukthebhirjarante tvāmaccā jaritāraḥ |
sutasomā aharvidā || 2 ||

## Translation
Vayu, the singers praise thee with hymns, they who have prepared the Soma and know the right seasons.

---

# Verse 3

## Sanskrit
वायो॑ त॒व प्र॑पृञ्च॒ती धेना॑ जिगाति दा॒शुषे॑ ।
उ॒रूची॑ सोम॒पीत॑ये ॥ ३ ॥

## IAST
vāyo tava prapṛñcatī dhenā jigāti dāśuṣe |
urūcī somapītaye || 3 ||

## Translation
Vayu, thy stream of speech goes to the giver of offerings, spreading wide to drink the Soma juice.
